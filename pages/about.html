Our Story

Empowering Innovation & Transformation

Established in 2022 by CEO Mr. Prashant Gaikwad, Xprowess Infotech Pvt. Ltd. is a premier Information Technology and Data Science services firm. With highly skilled professionals, we specialize in delivering superior data management, IT product development, and innovation & digital transformation services, web development, App development and many more.

Recognizing the industry’s technological gap, we embarked on a mission to drive digital transformation, developing cutting-edge IT solutions. Our commitment to excellence is underscored by our ISO 9001:2024 certification, reflecting our dedication to quality management.
