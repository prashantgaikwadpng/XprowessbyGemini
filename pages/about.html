Our Story

Empowering Innovation & Transformation

Xprowess Infotech Pvt Ltd is a technology consulting firm founded by CEO Prashant Gaikwad. We specialize in delivering superior data management, Business Intelligence, web development, mobile apps, and digital transformation solutions.

Recognizing the industry’s technological gap, we embarked on a mission to drive digital transformation, developing cutting-edge IT solutions grounded in real engineering expertise and a commitment to quality delivery.
